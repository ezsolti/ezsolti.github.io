Nuclear Hermitage

Welcome to the Nuclear Hermitage. This is a place for serendipity, study, and quiet focus, intended primarily for students and professionals in nuclear engineering and physics, or anyone working closely with nuclear topics. It is a space to read, think, and complete work that requires uninterrupted attention, such as a thesis or a journal paper. In this sense, it invites a temporary “hermit” mode, where one can withdraw from everyday distractions and work with the calm and discipline of a monk, while still remaining connected to a shared intellectual purpose. It can also host small, informal collaborations, such as focused discussions, project work, or a modest nuclear-themed hackathon.

The house has 7 to 8 beds, although guests may need to bring their own bedsheets. In practice, it is most suitable for a group of up to five people, as there is only one bathroom. The sleeping areas are not fully separated, and there are no proper doors between rooms, so privacy is limited. The space is therefore best suited for groups that are comfortable with a shared and open living environment for a short period of time.

Whereabouts

The hermitage is located 17 minutes bike ride from Höör (approachable by train from Copenhagen, Malmö). It takes 8 minutes by car from the station. There is also the possibility to take a bus from the station, and walk around 1.2km. The village is secluded, but is absolutely reachable without a car.

The perfect running trail is next to the cabin. The beautiful Sätoftasjön, with a fully equipped beach side, is within 10 minutes bike ride. The closest grocery store is 15 minutes.

There are some bikes at the property. The house is equipped to host a baby (travel crib and baby chair).
